title: mpDAO Grantees
---

Once a proposal has been awarded a grant from mpDAO, it is mandatory for the proposer to carry out their proposed activities and submit a report. This report will be made public for the Meta Pool community.

There are three stages to track the progress of the grant:

1. **Under Progress:** The grantee has received the funds and is either prepared to launch their project or is actively executing their proposal.
2. **Completed and Awaiting Report:** The proposal's activities have been successfully completed, but no report has been submitted to confirm this.
3. **Report Submitted:** The grantee has submitted the required report, and a link to this report is available for review.
