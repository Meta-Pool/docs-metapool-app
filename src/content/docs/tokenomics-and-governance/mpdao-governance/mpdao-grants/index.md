---
title: mpDAO Grants
---

mpDAO Grants represent a pivotal aspect of Meta Pool's commitment to community-driven governance and the realization of its vision for Liquid Staking and DeFi solutions. These grants serve as a structured avenue for supporting proposals that are in alignment with mpDAO goals.

The essence of mpDAO Grants lies in empowering the community to actively contribute to the growth and development of Meta Pool. They provide an opportunity for individuals and communities to propose innovative ideas, projects, or initiatives that further enhance the Liquid Staking and DeFi ecosystem within the Meta Pool network.

By offering financial support and resources through mpDAO Grants, Meta Pool aims to foster a vibrant and dynamic community who share the same passion for Liquid Staking and DeFi. These grants not only provide the means to turn ideas into reality but also contribute to the decentralization and democratization within the Meta Pool ecosystem.
