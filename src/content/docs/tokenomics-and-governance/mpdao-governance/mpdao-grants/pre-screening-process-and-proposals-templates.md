All grant proposals undergo a pre-screening process led by the Meta Pool Contributors team. This initial review ensures only high-quality proposals move to the voting stage, aligning with key performance indicators (KPIs) and the expected budget for each funding round to maximize mpDAO's resources.

The transparent criteria for this process are detailed in the decision matrix, which can be accessed here:

[AI + Blockchain decision Matrix](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Tyh9Hzivkr2-hTV3dl5sFBcj26LSez1KGPa8m7JvKM8/edit?gid=1179144580#gid=1179144580).

_Inspired in the Decision Matrix created by_ [_Dfinity Foundation_](https://dfinity.org/) _for their Grants Program._
