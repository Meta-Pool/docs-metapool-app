Metapool is a DAO dedicated to promote decentralized tools to empower sovereign individuals and financial freedom.

The [Meta Pool app](https://www.metapool.app) is where the DAO presents the products and activities below. The landing identifies the Meta Pool DAO, since 2021, and offers three paths: stake, vote, and build. Yields and staking totals there are live. Use the app for current numbers, and this page for what the DAO maintains.

The landing publishes three running measures of staking activity: total value locked, the number of stakers, and staking rewards distributed. Read the current figures on [metapool.app](https://www.metapool.app) or [Meta Pool stats](https://stats.metapool.app).

## Build Buddies

[Build Buddies](https://buildbuddies.metapool.app/) is a global community meetup and networking initiative powered by Meta Pool. It connects local developers, founders, and crypto enthusiasts across Latin America and Asia through in-person gatherings.

Cities named on the landing include Santiago de Chile, Mojokerto, Sidoarjo, Surabaya, Saigon, Hanoi, Mendoza, Buenos Aires, and Ciudad Victoria.

The meetups are organized around:

* **Networking.** Bringing Web3 builders and curious newcomers together in person.
* **Education.** Sharing information about the blockchain revolution.
* **Collaboration.** Providing inclusive spaces where people can pitch grant-ready ideas.

## Metapool Incubator

The Metapool Incubator is for builders working alongside the Meta Pool DAO. Each project is selected through curated review, tracked through weekly milestones, and integrated into the DAO's distribution channels. The incubator is on the [Meta Pool landing](https://www.metapool.app/#projects).

**Cohort 01 / 2026** on the landing lists two active projects, and that section marks applications as open. Confirm the current cohort on the landing before treating this list as fixed.

* **[IntentFolio](https://intentfolio.app)** (intentfolio.app) — finance, portfolio, and DeFi, marked beta. IntentFolio lets you set a target mix across assets, then keeps the funds automatically managed and rebalanced as markets move.
* **[Bitju](https://bitju.app)** (Bitju.app) — streaming, gaming, and multichain, marked deployed. Bitju turns creators and their audiences into shared economies, where supporting a streamer means owning a piece of their growth.

## We vote

From validator fees to treasury allocation, every meaningful decision moves through an on-chain Meta Pool Improvement Proposal (MPIP). Hold mpDAO. Vote. Propose.

Three ways to participate, as the landing describes them:

1. **Get mpDAO, vote, and earn.** Start with [How to get mpDAO](./meta-pool-ecosystem/vote-governance/how-to-get-mpdao/), [Vote to Earn](./welcome-to-meta-pool/vote-to-earn/), and [Vote-to-Earn rewards](./tokenomics-and-governance/mpdao-governance/vote-to-earn-rewards/).
2. **Vote on active MPIPs** at [metapool.app/mpip](https://www.metapool.app/mpip). [Meta Pool Improvement Proposals](./meta-pool-ecosystem/vote-governance/meta-pool-improvement-proposals-mpips/) covers how proposals are created and how the community votes.
3. **Allocate stake to validators via DAO voting.** See [Vote for Validators](./meta-pool-ecosystem/vote-governance/vote-for-validators/) and [How stake distribution works](./meta-pool-ecosystem/vote-governance/how-stake-distribution-works/).

The landing's governance app is [main.metapool.app/en/vote](https://main.metapool.app/en/vote). Broader context is in [Vote / Governance](./meta-pool-ecosystem/vote-governance/) and [mpDAO Governance](./tokenomics-and-governance/mpdao-governance/).

The landing also lists live and recently passed MPIPs. Fee and distribution terms are defined in [MPIP-32, Additional Buyback-and-Burn Program](https://www.metapool.app/mpip?id=32).
