mpDAO (Meta Pool's Decentralized Autonomous Organization) is a community-driven governance system at the heart of Meta Pool's ecosystem. Designed to empower users worldwide, mpDAO fosters transparency, inclusivity, and decentralization.&#x20;

Through mpDAO, community actively participate in decision-making, proposal approvals, and resource allocation, shaping the future of Meta Pool's liquid staking and DeFi solutions.&#x20;

Meta Pool's journey towards becoming a DAO is an intricate process, far from a simple endeavor. The dedicated [Meta Pool contributors ](contributors-team.md)are collaborating closely with the community, governance token holders, and fellow contributors to navigate this complex path.&#x20;

Learn more about it in the [Tokenomics & Governance](https://app.gitbook.com/s/5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt/tokenomics-and-governance) section.
