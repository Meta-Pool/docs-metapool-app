## Vote to Earn

All active voters **GET PAID** for the work of participating in the governance. Protocol fees are distributed each month to active voters (those with locked tokens and with used voting power). The current distribution is defined in [MPIP-32](https://www.metapool.app/mpip?id=32).

The payment is proportional to the voting power used during the month. Payments are distributed on the third day of the next month.
