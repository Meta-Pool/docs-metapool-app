title: Enterprise Node Operators Program
---

The Enterprise Node Operators (ENO) program is designed for large companies that wish to begin their journey into blockchain through the NEAR Protocol.

ENO participants operate professional-grade validators, typically backed by robust infrastructure, technical expertise, and a long-term commitment to the network.

The program’s goal is to onboard enterprises — both traditional and tech-based — into the Web3 space, allowing them to directly engage with NEAR’s governance, staking economy, and NEAR ecosystem products.

### Current and past Participants

These companies currently participate or have participated in the ENO Program:<br>

* NTT DATA – Global IT services and infrastructure provider
* Deutsche Telekom – Multinational telecommunications company
* Vodafone / PairPoint – Global telecom and enterprise blockchain services
* Blockdeamon – Blockchain infrastructure and node operations provider
* Luganodes – Institutional-grade staking provider
* Colossus Digital – Institutional-grade blockchain infrastructure provider enabling secure, compliant staking and yield access for financial institutions.
* Staking4all – Professional staking and validator services
* Everstake – Global non-custodial staking provider
* BCW – Blockchain infrastructure and validator operator
* Nansen – Blockchain analytics and on-chain data platform

The Enterprise Node Operators (ENO) program is aimed at large, globally recognized companies interested in beginning their blockchain journey through the NEAR Protocol.\
We are particularly looking for organizations in sectors such as telecommunications, technology, cloud infrastructure, and fintech, as well as other enterprises with the capacity and interest to operate a blockchain validator at scale.

### How to Join

Companies that wish to join should submit a proposal describing:

* Their organizational profile and infrastructure capabilities.
* Experience in blockchain, distributed systems, or cloud operations.
* A commitment to maintaining high uptime and operational transparency.
* Their motivation and goals for participating in the NEAR ecosystem.<br>
