title: Wrapped $stNEAR
---

$stNEAR co lives in the Aurora Network through the Rainbow Bridge on its wrapped version. The Wrapped $stNEAR allows the Aurora Network users to keep the benefits of the $stNEAR on an EVM compatible network.

The rest of the information can be seen at [What is $stNEAR?](../near-protocol/what-is-usdstnear.md) section.
