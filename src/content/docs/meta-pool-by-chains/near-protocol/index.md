NEAR Protocol is a blockchain platform that aims to make decentralized technology accessible to everyone. It combines the power of blockchain with a user-friendly experience, making it easy for developers and users alike to engage with the world of decentralized applications (dApps). With NEAR, you don't need to be a technical wizard to participate in the blockchain revolution.

NEAR Protocol excels in scalability, a critical feature often lacking in traditional blockchains. Traditional blockchains struggle to handle large transaction volumes, resulting in slow processing times and high fees.

#### Stake NEAR with Meta Pool

The primary way to liquid-stake NEAR with Meta Pool is through [metapool.app](https://www.metapool.app) — mint **$stNEAR**, use DeFi with your LST, and manage unstake flows in the current Meta Pool UI.

Child pages in this section cover what $stNEAR is, how Meta Pool works on NEAR, why stake with Meta Pool, and validator selection.

#### Historical note: BOS / NEAR Social widgets

In 2023, NEAR’s core team announced the Blockchain Operating System (BOS), a decentralized frontend mechanism. Meta Pool published staking widgets on NEAR Social that you may still find online:

* [Validators](https://near.social/#/meta-pool-official.near/widget/MetaStakingVote)
* [Stake ETH](https://near.social/meta-pool-official.near/widget/MetaPoolStakeEth) — see also Liquid Staking in BOS (historical guide)

Prefer [metapool.app](https://www.metapool.app) for current stake and vote flows; treat BOS/NEAR Social paths as historical alternatives.
