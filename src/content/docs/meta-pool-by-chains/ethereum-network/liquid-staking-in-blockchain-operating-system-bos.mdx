### NEAR Blockchain Operating System (BOS)

With the decision to bring the Ethereum and NEAR communities closer together, Meta Pool developed a widget to stake ETH and receive spETH on the NEAR Blockchain Operating System (BOS) / NEAR Social.

#### How does it work? (BOS widget)

1.  Go to [https://near.social/meta-pool-official.near/widget/MetaPoolStakeEth](https://near.social/meta-pool-official.near/widget/MetaPoolStakeEth).

    <figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FDsQtOo4mtw1MK8JZ9uk4%2Fimagen.png?alt=media&#x26;token=fe10bd0e-9177-42af-92c4-a495e18c9098" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2. **Connect your wallet** by clicking the Connect with Ethereum wallet button.
3.  **Choose your preferred wallet**.

    <figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FKHHTg7tKCzOJhSHZWtXn%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=7739292e-0db1-46ad-b0be-0c1a13876818" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4.  **Enter the amount** that you wish to stake, then click '_**Stake ETH**_' button.

    <figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FqCTtFd1kCZ6M5y49FaHe%2Fimagen.png?alt=media&#x26;token=214635e9-0e54-4da1-8038-f783e126f3cc" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
5.  "_**Confirm**_" the transaction in your wallet.

    <figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FKBZUhS3H5AkPuVcECqUD%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=864060da-12d0-41a1-98c3-ee2ccc68bc42" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
6.  Congratulations, you are now an spETH holder. Keep staking and earning rewards.

    <figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FCEsjH10E7NcbJS79ruPs%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=e64d34ea-e21e-4b0f-b085-53b31afcc431" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
