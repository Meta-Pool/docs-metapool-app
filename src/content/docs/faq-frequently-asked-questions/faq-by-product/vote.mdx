<Aside type="danger">

**Deprecated:** mpDAO on Ethereum is deprecated. Use mpDAO on **NEAR** ([metapool.app/vote](https://www.metapool.app/vote)). Ethereum mpDAO details on this page are historical.

</Aside>


<details>

<summary>What is mpDAO?</summary>

mpDAO is the Governance token of Meta Pool. It allows holders of mpDAO tokens to be part of Meta Pool's decisions. Being a holder of mpDAO makes you a participant in the Meta Pool DAO.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How much will mpDAO cost?</summary>

mpDAO price depends on the market. To obtain mpDAO on NEAR, follow these instructions. mpDAO on Ethereum is deprecated.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Does Meta Pool governance work on Ethereum? (Deprecated)</summary>

No. **mpDAO on Ethereum is deprecated.** From mid-May 2024, Ethereum wallets could be linked to a NEAR wallet so that mpDAO locked on Ethereum could vote through NEAR; that Ethereum path is no longer supported. Meta Pool governance is managed on NEAR: get, lock and vote with mpDAO on NEAR at [metapool.app/vote](https://www.metapool.app/vote).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Will Meta Pool governance stop being on NEAR?</summary>

No, the governance of Meta Pool remains on NEAR. All the votes required for governance are conducted on the NEAR network. mpDAO on Ethereum is deprecated.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How can I obtain mpDAO?</summary>

Get mpDAO on NEAR — directly from [metapool.app/vote](https://www.metapool.app/vote) or on Rhea Finance. Here is how to get mpDAO. Acquiring mpDAO on Ethereum (Uniswap) is deprecated.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What can I vote on to earn rewards?</summary>

Meta Pool provides different voting options. You can vote on Validators, Grants, and MPIPs. Voting is done with Voting Power on NEAR. (mpDAO on Ethereum is deprecated.)

</details>

<details>

<summary>What should I do to earn rewards?</summary>

To earn rewards, it's important to participate in the Meta Pool DAO. You can participate in the Meta Pool DAO by voting. The current voting possibilities are: Validators, Grants, and MPIPs.

Here is more information: [My votes](../../meta-pool-ecosystem/vote-governance/my-votes/).

</details>

<details>

<summary>When can I withdraw my rewards?</summary>

Rewards are calculated daily, but they are available for withdrawal every 3rd day of each month.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How can I bridge mpDAO between NEAR blockchain and Ethereum blockchain? (Deprecated)</summary>

**mpDAO on Ethereum is deprecated**, so bridging mpDAO to Ethereum is not recommended. Historically, [bitju.app](https://bitju.app/) was listed as a bridge to move mpDAO between NEAR and Ethereum. Use mpDAO on NEAR for governance.

</details>
