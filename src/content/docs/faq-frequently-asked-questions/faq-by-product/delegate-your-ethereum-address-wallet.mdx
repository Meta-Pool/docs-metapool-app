<Aside type="danger">

**Deprecated — mpDAO on Ethereum.** mpDAO on Ethereum (the Ethereum ERC-20 token, the Uniswap pool, locking mpDAO / Voting Power on Ethereum, and delegating an Ethereum address to a NEAR account) is **deprecated**. Do not use it for new purchases, locks or votes. Meta Pool governance runs on **NEAR**: get, lock and vote with mpDAO on NEAR at [metapool.app/vote](https://www.metapool.app/vote). Any Ethereum details below are kept for historical reference only.

</Aside>


<details>

<summary>How can I delegate my Ethereum account to a NEAR account?</summary>

In this link, we explain to you step by step how to Delegate your Ethereum account.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Accounts from which network need to be delegated in order to vote and receive payments for voting?</summary>

Ethereum accounts must be delegated and represented by a NEAR account in order to vote and receive payments for voting. Keep in mind that to receive payments for your Voting Power, you must have voted for any of the voting options the Meta Pool DAO has. More info [here](../../meta-pool-ecosystem/vote-governance/my-votes/).

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why do I need to delegate my Ethereum account?</summary>

To be able to vote with your Voting Powers in the Meta Pool DAO, which operates on the NEAR network, you must delegate the representation of your Ethereum accounts to a NEAR account.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is the purpose of delegating my Ethereum account?</summary>

The purpose of delegating your Ethereum account is to participate in the Meta Pool DAO that operates on NEAR. Participation in the DAO involves voting. By voting with your Voting Power, you can receive payments every month.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is the minimum amount of Voting Power I need to have to delegate my Ethereum account?</summary>

The minimum requirement is 1000 Voting Power in your Ethereum account to delegate the representation to a NEAR account.

</details>

<details>

<summary>I cannot vote with my Voting Power from your Ethereum account. What happens?</summary>

Voting cannot be done from the Ethereum account. To vote, you must delegate your representation to a NEAR account. Once the delegation is done, make sure to choose the NEAR network and that it is representing the Ethereum account. This way, you can vote and receive payments for voting without any issues.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How do I receive my payments for voting in the Meta Pool DAO?</summary>

Payments for voting in the Meta Pool DAO are made on the NEAR network in the Representative account. These payments are in stNEAR, and on the 3rd of each month, payments are credited to the account, and you can claim them.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why does my Ethereum account to be represented end with evmp.near?</summary>

The reason your Ethereum account, to be represented, ends with _**evmp.near**_ is because it's actually a mirrored Ethereum account that allows you to vote on NEAR. This way, you can keep your assets on the Ethereum network and participate in the Meta Pool DAO without any issues.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Select the correct NEAR and Ethereum connected accounts.</summary>

Keep in mind that Meta Pool is multichain. Therefore, you can have an Ethereum account and a NEAR account connected, and the connected Ethereum account might not be represented by the connected NEAR account. Make sure you are certain about which accounts are connected.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I delegate more than one Ethereum account to a NEAR account?</summary>

**A NEAR account can represent many Ethereum accounts.**

</details>
