<summary>What’s the crypto economic structure of Meta Pool?</summary>

In Meta Pool value is captured in three ways:&#x20;

1. Fees that come from staking rewards in multiple chains.&#x20;
2. Liquid Unstaking fees which will be variable \~0.3% - 3% (depends on existing liquidity and operation amount, similar to standard swap pools).&#x20;
3. The revenue generated on the platform from staking and liquidity pool fees is sent to the Treasury and handled by the smart contract in the form of stNEAR tokens.
4. Most of the protocol fees (60%) are paid to mpDAO holders for voting in the governance. See [Vote to Earn](../../welcome-to-meta-pool/vote-to-earn.md)

</details>

<summary>What rewards do I get in Meta Pool?</summary>

In Meta Pool you will receive payment for voting (protocol fees, stNEAR) every month, if you're a mpDAO holder and vote to direct the protocol evolution. See [Vote to Earn](../../welcome-to-meta-pool/vote-to-earn.md)

Also liquid staking means you receive a liquid staking token when you stake and contribute to the blockchain security (NEAR, Ethereum, Solana). All our liquid staking tokens (stNEAR, spETH, mpSOL, stICP) increase in value every epoch representing the rewards gained by staking. See [stats.metapool.app](https://stats.metapool.app) for our open stats about liquid staking and governance.

</details>

<details>

<summary>How does Meta Pool selects validators in the NEAR chain?</summary>

Validators are staked if they have: (1) an uptime >95%, (2) fee of <10%, (3) not being over the Nakamoto Coefficient and (4) have enough tokens to accomplish seat price of NEAR protocol.

Also 25% of the stake is decide by mpDAO holders voting. You can check the validators vote section at [https://www.metapool.app/stakevote/?network=near](https://www.metapool.app/stakevote/?network=near)
