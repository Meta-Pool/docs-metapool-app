title: FAQ Community Representatives

<details>

<summary>What is a community representative?</summary>

A community representative is an individual who voluntarily presents an initiative proposal to Meta Pool's DAO. These individuals typically lead communities or organizations and are seeking funding. They share a common vision with Meta Pool, aligning their goals with those of the protocol and decentralized finances.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Which are the requirements for being considered a community representative?</summary>

To be eligible as a community representative, one must be a publicly recognized individual who has gained a positive reputation within the community and presents an initiative proposal to the DAO.

Meta Pool may utilize the i-am-human platform, a proof-of-personhood platform, to verify the identity of individuals.

</details>

<details>

<summary>What is the difference between community representatives and ambassadors?</summary>

Ambassadors are part of an internal program to widespread Meta Pool contents worldwide. A community representative can be any individual leading a community.

</details>
