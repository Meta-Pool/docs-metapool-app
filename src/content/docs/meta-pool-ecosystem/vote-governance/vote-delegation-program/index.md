---
title: Vote Delegation Program
---

The **Delegation Program** allows **mpDAO** holders to delegate their **Voting Power (VP)** to registered community **Delegates**. This enables participation in governance without manually voting on each proposal, validator, or grant.

Delegates vote using the combined VP (their own + delegated from others), and delegators share in the generated rewards.

**Important**: Delegating VP does **not** mean losing control of your locked **mpDAO** or wallet. You can change or withdraw your delegation at any time.

**What do you get by delegating?**

* **Base voting rewards** (monthly): Rewards from the Vote-to-Earn program, based on VP used in effective votes (validators and grants). Distributed on the **3rd day of each month**. The Delegate sets a **fee** percentage (typically 0%-50%, up to 100%) deducted from these rewards; the remaining portion goes directly to you as the delegator.
* **Extra farming rewards** (daily): Many Delegates create **Farms** with additional incentives (e.g., USDC, USDT, NEAR, stNEAR, mpDAO). If the Delegate has an active Farm and this Delegate meets the minimum VP requirement set by the Delegate, you receive a proportional share daily. Rewards are claimable in the Delegation Program section.

**How to choose a Delegate**

Visit the **Delegate Votes** page to browse the list of registered Delegates. The table includes:

* **Nickname** and **Account** (NEAR address, often with verification badge or checkmark for endorsed ones).
* **Opportunity**: Shows the current Farm status.
  * **Rewards ON** (Active - green) if the Delegate's minimum total VP requirement is met → Farm is distributing rewards.
  * **Rewards OFF** (Inactive - red) if min VP not reached, with **Missing XX VP** displayed.
  * Farm size/token (e.g., Farm: 100 USDT).
* **Farming Rewards**: Token and period (Farm Start / Farm End dates).
* **Fee**: The Delegate's commission % on base voting rewards.
* **Voting Rewards**: Pending fee changes (if any) and **Effective at** date (changes have a delay to aware delegators).
* **Action**: Delegate button to start the process.

Delegates can only have **one active Farm** at a time (consecutive farms). To see upcoming or finished Farms, click the Delegate's row to open their detailed overview (includes self VP, received VP, farm history, fee management, etc.).

Use the search bar to filter by nickname or account. Click a Delegate's nickname to view full details, including slider simulation for estimating your potential farming rewards based on delegated VP.
