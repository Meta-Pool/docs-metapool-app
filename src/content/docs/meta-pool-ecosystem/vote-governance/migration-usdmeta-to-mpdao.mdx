<Aside type="caution">

**Historical (2024):** This page documents events from April–May 2024 ($META → mpDAO migration and/or vote reset). It is kept for reference only. Deadlines have passed; this is not a current how-to. For live governance docs, see [Vote / Governance](./) and Voting Power Calculator.

</Aside>


### **Points to consider before migrating from $META to mpDAO.**

1. To start the migration, you needed your _**NEAR wallet connected**_.
2. You needed _**all your positions locked**_. Positions in Unlock blocked migration.
3. You needed to ensure you _**did not have any position in Withdraw**_. Withdraw positions did not block migration, but left $META in the wallet that could not be migrated.
4. _**Lock all your $META tokens**_ before migrating. Unlocked $META in the wallet could not be migrated with that wallet.

<Aside type="danger">

Very important: verify before migrating that you have no pending Withdraw positions, and that all your $META are locked. Once you perform the migration, it will not be possible to perform a new Migration with $META pending to be locked.

</Aside>
