#### Governance Platform of Meta Pool (context from May 2024)

**First of all, we would like to provide you with some clarifications:**

1. **Migration from $META to $mpDAO:** Users who had not migrated $META to $mpDAO were directed to: [old.metapool.app](https://old.metapool.app/). And follow the instructions: Migration to mpDAO.
2. The acquisition of mpDAO on [UNISWAP](https://app.uniswap.org/) was enabled on the **Ethereum** network (mpDAO on Ethereum is now **deprecated**). Click here to know how to get mpDAO.
3. For more information, you can go to our FAQ section or contact Meta Pool on Discord.

**Prop 3 - Governance Tokenomics Reset**

On October 14, 2023, enhancement proposal number 3 (mpIP-3) was approved. Which postulates the migration of the $META token in order to introduce Multichain Governance, having a native Governance token in Ethereum and NEAR. Thereby welcoming $mpDAO as the new governance token. mpIP-3 proposed in NEAR Gov.

* mpIP-3: [Prop 3 - Governance Tokenomics Reset](https://dev.near.org/meta-pool-official.near/widget/ImprovementProposals?tab=proposal\&mpip_id=3)
* Article on Medium: [Process of implementing MPIP #3: Governance Tokenomics Reset](https://medium.com/meta-pool/process-of-implementing-mpip-3-governance-tokenomics-reset-b4456d3a0d29)

#### Meta Pool DAO - New UI

Log in to [metapool.app](https://www.metapool.app/) and go to the Vote section. Or directly click [here](https://www.metapool.app/vote/).

**Voting Power Calculator**

Meta Pool rewards Meta Pool Dao participants (mpDAO Holders). Meta Pool rewards Meta Pool DAO participants (mpDAO holders).

**Connect your wallet to lock your mpDAO tokens and get Voting Power**

Meta Pool is Multichain, so first choose the network you wish to connect to.

<figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FLWdkLuAo9puMhbEacA72%2Fimagen.png?alt=media&#x26;token=c95f5de8-f573-49f9-a126-76e348aab749" alt="" /><figcaption><p>Chose between NEAR or Ethereum Network to participate in the Meta Pool DAO.</p></figcaption></figure>


<Aside type="note">

To participate in Meta Pool Governance and receive rewards, you can connect to the <mark style="color:blue;">**NEAR**</mark> network and/or the <mark style="color:blue;">**Ethereum**</mark> network.

</Aside>


Once you have chosen the network you want, connect your wallet so that you can have full access to your assets.

<figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FtzJiZeor8DeixFyFyTpZ%2Fimagen.png?alt=media&#x26;token=fd8e7976-deff-4231-9333-af8d7ee09bbb" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can have 1 wallet connected per network. To operate with each network, you will have to change network each time in the selector at the top right of the metapoo.app page.

<figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FXGKabQcwQRmMK4htTTzk%2Fimagen.png?alt=media&#x26;token=54d8665d-7ec0-47bf-b5dc-f55ae4ef3ef6" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Congratulations, your wallet is connected. You can now participate in the Meta Pool DAO.

**How to get Voting Power**

Now that your wallet is connected, you will see that in the calculator, the button that used to say "+ Connect your Wallet", now says "<mark style="color:blue;">**Lock your mpDAO now**</mark>".

Therefore, to get Voting Power you can click on "[<mark style="color:blue;">**Lock your mpDAO now**</mark>](my-votes/)" or go to the [<mark style="color:blue;">**My Votes**</mark>](my-votes/) section.
