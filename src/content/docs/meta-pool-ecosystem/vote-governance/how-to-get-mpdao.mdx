<Aside type="danger">

**Deprecated:** mpDAO on Ethereum is deprecated. Use mpDAO on **NEAR** ([metapool.app/vote](https://www.metapool.app/vote)). Ethereum mpDAO details on this page are historical.

</Aside>


**mpDAO can be purchased in the following ways:**

* Directly from Meta Pool (NEAR networks) - (Locked mpDAO and get voting Power).
* Rhea finance (NEAR network)
* ~~Uniswap (Ethereum network)~~ — **Deprecated**: mpDAO on Ethereum is no longer supported (see note below).

### Meta Pool - Vote

1\) Go to [Meta Pool - Vote](https://www.metapool.app/vote)

2\) Connect your wallet (NEAR)

**Select between this tokens** to purchase and locked mpDAO. O directly locked your mpDAOs.

### Ethereum network (Deprecated)


<Aside type="danger">

**Deprecated — mpDAO on Ethereum.** mpDAO on Ethereum (the Ethereum ERC-20 token, the Uniswap pool, locking mpDAO / Voting Power on Ethereum, and delegating an Ethereum address to a NEAR account) is **deprecated**. Do not use it for new purchases, locks or votes. Meta Pool governance runs on **NEAR**: get, lock and vote with mpDAO on NEAR at [metapool.app/vote](https://www.metapool.app/vote). Any Ethereum details below are kept for historical reference only.

</Aside>
