### mpDAO Vote

mpDAO Vote is the governance tool for the Meta Pool DAO, where token holders define:

* Which Grants to fund (monthly protocol fees)
* Which validators should receive extra stake (25% of the total NEAR stake)
* Which project to launch in the Launchpad (Returning soon)
* What proposals should be considered. MPIPs (Meta Pool Improvement Proposals).
* (Soon) How the mpINDEX is composed

### How to use mpDAO Vote?

You can access mpDAO Vote directly from its link [www.metapool.app/vote](https://www.metapool.app/vote).

### mpDAO Vote works with 3 simple steps:

1. Lock your mpDAO tokens
2. Get voting power
3. Vote for validators, Grants, MPIPs, or projects in the Launchpad to get rewards.

#### How to get Voting Power

In order to be able to vote you must obtain Voting Power and it is very simple. The way to do it is by **locking your mpDAO tokens**.

### Lock stages

#### Lock Position

The Lock stage is the one that allows you to vote on Meta Pool DAO. mpDAO tokens are locked and users can vote for projects or validators at any time. _There is no time limit for this stage_. Voting power will be available as long as mpDAO tokens are locked.

<figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FLj7368zh8TrIEWI1ZQDT%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=3ce92f0b-1c1f-4e6a-b4bf-fd0e6f535b3d" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Unbonding Period

If you decide to stop participating in the DAO and remove your tokens or partially remove your tokens, you can start the unbonding period. By pressing the "Start Unbonding" button in a locked position, _the unboding period will start._ The unboding period is the number of days selected at locking time.

<figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2Fz2yiYN0YOEnkQIs1js5O%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=72a58f8b-c012-4e4c-a74f-4a51970b3924" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Important: the ubonding period does not start automatically, it must be manually started by the user by clicking "Start Unbonding".

If there is voting power in use and not enough free voting power to unbond a full position, you will not be able to start the unbonding period. Remove some of the votes to start the unbonding period.\
\
**Reminder**: It is not necessary that the total amount of time chosen for the $mpDAO unbonding period has passed. You can start the unbonding process whenever you want or need, even if only two hours or two days have passed.\

#### Relock

At any time during the unbonding period, you can relock your position and gain the voting power corresponding to the days left. You can use the votes and then resume the unbonding period later.

<figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2F1CAQSKOaqwTDpOQ2dq8o%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=eab95183-a842-436e-b3d9-e296832b6f86" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
