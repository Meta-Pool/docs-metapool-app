### Voting with Voting Power in NEAR network

1. If you don't have Voting Power yet, connect your NEAR wallet to lock your mpDAO tokens and get Voting Power (see [here](../#how-to-use-mpdao-vote) how to lock your mpDAO).
2. Go to the Meta Pool product you want to vote for. Vote for validators, Grants, [MPIPs](../meta-pool-improvement-proposals-mpips/), or projects in the Launchpad (_Returning soon_) to get rewards.

You are already participating with your vote in Meta Pool DAO.

**You can see your voted positions in My Votes tab from My Votes section.**

Do not use it for new purchases, locks or votes. Meta Pool governance runs on **NEAR**: get, lock and vote with mpDAO on NEAR at [metapool.app/vote](https://www.metapool.app/vote). 3. Vote with your Ethereum mpDAO with your NEAR representative account.
4. Go to the Meta Pool product you want to vote for. Vote for validators, Grants, [MPIPs](../meta-pool-improvement-proposals-mpips/), or projects in the Launchpad to get rewards.

### **Refresh your Votes**

#### Phase 1: Vote Position Timestamp

The first phase of the **mpip-16** development added to all voting positions the corresponding date on which the transaction that originates the voting position is signed. With this new data included in our contract, we can now perform a cleanup of DAO participants’ voting positions whose age exceeds the 60-day threshold.

#### Phase 2: Refresh Functionality

The second phase of the **mpip-16** development added the **“refresh”** functionality to the contract, allowing DAO participants to revalidate their voting positions on Validators. This updates the issue date of this type of voting position and prevents it from being cleaned up due to age.

> ⚠️ Voting positions on **grants & memes** cannot be revalidated.

#### Phase 3: Cleanup Bot

The third phase of the **mpip-16** development consisted of creating a bot that runs once a day, scanning all voting positions and deleting those older than the 60-day threshold.


<Aside type="note">

All voting positions were updated with an issue date of **08/01/2025**. Therefore, after 60 days from that date, the votes will be deleted (with the possibility of revalidating voting positions on Validators).

</Aside>
