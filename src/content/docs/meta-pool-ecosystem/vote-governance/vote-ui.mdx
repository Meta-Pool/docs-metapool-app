#### Voting Power Calculator

<figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FFaWbyH6Dk5GpSRAENjWB%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=4cd6eb6e-d80b-4dec-9394-91144b7eec44" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Meta Pool provides you with a tool to calculate the Voting Power and the rewards to receive if you deposite tokens, get mpDAO, lock your mpDAO tokens and vote with the acquired Voting Power.


<Aside type="note">

Meta Pool rewards Meta Pool Dao participants (mpDAO Holders). Don't miss the opportunity of being part of Meta Pool Dao.

</Aside>


#### Connect your NEAR wallet to lock your mpDAO tokens and get Voting Power

Choose the **NEAR** network in the network selector and connect your NEAR wallet.

<figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FDOaLtZWIPRQBoEJloooC%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=fa2ee536-d603-49db-a0e9-6eef2a1b6715" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


<Aside type="note">

To participate in Meta Pool Governance and receive rewards, connect to the <mark style="color:blue;">**NEAR**</mark> network.

</Aside>



<Aside type="danger">

**Deprecated:** mpDAO on Ethereum is deprecated. Use mpDAO on **NEAR** ([metapool.app/vote](https://www.metapool.app/vote)). Ethereum mpDAO details on this page are historical.

</Aside>


Once you have chosen the network you want, connect your wallet so that you can have full access to your assets.

<figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FAk5Yt00Q8VBrmL7LM2MV%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=c74ecc65-f2ab-4ef0-b5db-f97400acb106" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2F3OohdytnTZE7Jy0yQwMd%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=aecf30ab-a58e-47b0-aa89-3bc6540b98f9" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can have 1 wallet connected per network. To operate with each network, you will have to change network each time in the selector at the top right of the metapoo.app page.

<figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FiZ6n8t8ckrQeYKrzSCH1%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=5c55b09a-fc12-4da6-a011-f02ceb9c197b" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Congratulations, your wallet is connected. You can now participate in the Meta Pool DAO.

#### How to get Voting Power

Now that your wallet is connected, you will see that in the calculator, the button that used to say "+ Connect your Wallet", now says "<mark style="color:blue;">**Lock your mpDAO now**</mark>".

<figure><img src="https://1322102881-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F5HvxIwoS8YhIHItpA1lt%2Fuploads%2FwvCb7WhVEyWekYNZ49BV%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=5a0243d8-b981-4541-ad10-f05e16bfd411" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Therefore, to get Voting Power you can click on "[<mark style="color:blue;">**Lock your mpDAO now**</mark>](my-votes/)" or go to the [<mark style="color:blue;">**My Votes**</mark>](my-votes/) section.
