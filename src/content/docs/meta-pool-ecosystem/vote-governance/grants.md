title: Grants

The grants are provided aiming to secure funding from the mpDAO treasury. Grants have a general objective of fostering growth for Meta Pool, with the following specific objectives:

* Education in DeFi/Blockchain.
* TVL generation.
* Brand Awareness.
* AI

Applying for a grant request community representatives to have a clear execution process and KPIs for their initiatives and should express their interest to the Meta Pool team.

Read more about how to apply for a grant here: [mpdao-grants](../../tokenomics-and-governance/mpdao-governance/mpdao-grants/ "mention")

### Grants when voting period is active

mpDAO members can vote for an active proposal of a grant if there is an active period.

Users should claim votes on [my-votes](my-votes/ "mention") section and later pick a proposal to be voted assigning its voting power.
