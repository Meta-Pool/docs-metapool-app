## How to Withdraw your Unstake ticket

Note: To withdraw your mpSOL and receive your LST (mSOL, jitoSOL, hubSOL or bSOL) you must first go to Unstake to create a Withdraw ticket.

## Tickets Pending to Withdraw

You can have several Withdraw tickets pending and/or ready to withdraw.

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2857%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can view the **status of your ticket** and the **remaining time** to make the withdrawal.&#x20;

After the specified time has passed, you can execute **the withdrawal**.&#x20;

## Tickets Ready to Withdraw

Once the waiting period has expired, the ticket is ready for Withdraw.

You can have several Withdraw tickets running and ready to withdraw.

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%28251%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1\) Click on the **Withdraw** Button to select the withdraw options.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%28252%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2\) Select the Token (LST Token - mSOL, jitoSOL, hubSOL or bSOL) to withdraw.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2851%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


<Aside type="note">

**Important:** you must check the maximums available in order to withdraw the chosen LST token.

</Aside>


3\) Enter the Amount of SOL Value you want to withdraw from this ticket.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2852%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4\) The system informs you of the amount in the chosen LST to be received (equivalent to the SOL value you selected).

<figure><img src="/assets/imagen%20%28125%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5\) Click the **Withdraw button** and approve the transaction.

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2853%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6\) Congratulations, you correctly Unstaked mpSOL in Meta Pool.&#x20;
