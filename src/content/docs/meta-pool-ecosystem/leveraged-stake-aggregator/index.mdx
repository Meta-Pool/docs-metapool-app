mpSOL is the first Solana Leveraged Stake Aggregator. Meta Pool allows you to obtain extra yield from SOL or LSTs in Solana, like:  mSOL, jitoSOL, hubSOL, bSOL and receive mpSOL. (Go to [FAQ](../../faq-frequently-asked-questions/faq-by-product/solana-leveraged-stake-faq.md) section for more information)

## To Stake follow these steps

1\) Go to [https://www.metapool.app/stake](https://www.metapool.app/stake)&#x20;

2\) **LST-Token selection:** Choose $SOL or the LST-token of your preference. You need to have $SOL and the LST tokens in your Solana wallet to stake.

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2854%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


<Aside type="note">

**You can simulate a transaction, without having to connect your wallet.**

</Aside>


3\) **Connect your wallet** by clicking the Connect wallet button.

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2855%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4\) **Choose your preferred Solana wallet**.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2856%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


<Aside type="note">

If you have other Solana wallets already present in your browser, they will be recognized and you will be able to connect them as well (example: Phantom).

</Aside>


5\) **Enter the amount** that you wish to (Leverage) Stake, then click '**Stake**'


<Aside type="note">

Note, the **minimum** you are allowed to deposit is 0.01 units

</Aside>



<Aside type="caution">

Note: always leave some SOL on your wallet to cover transaction costs.

</Aside>


6\) **Stake**: click on the "**Stake**" button and then "_**Approve**_" or "_**Confirm**_" the transaction in your wallet.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%28245%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%28246%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


<Aside type="note">

You'll get **confirmation** after the transaction has been processed by the blockchain and your deposit has been successful. This usually only _**takes a few seconds**_.

</Aside>


7\) **Dashboard**: Now you can find your accounts holdings on the Dashboard section. [https://www.metapool.app/dashboard](https://www.metapool.app/dashboard)
