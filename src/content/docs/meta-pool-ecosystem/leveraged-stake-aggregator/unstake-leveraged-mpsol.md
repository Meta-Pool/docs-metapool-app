## Steps to follow for Unstake leveraged mpSOL

If you want to **unstake** your **mpSOL to get a normal Liquid Staked Token: mSOL, BSOL, HubSOL or JitoSOL,** just go to [https://www.metapool.app/](https://www.metapool.app/) an then select SOL or a SOL LST and select Unstake.

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%28249%29.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

To Unstake simply:

1\) Enter the **amount of the mpSOL** you wish to unrestake.

The system will inform you the amount you will receive in SOL Value.

2\) It will also inform you of the estimated waiting time for the withdrawal.

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%28250%29.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3\) Click the **Unstake button** and approve the transaction in your wallet.

* After a few seconds, your transaction has been confirmed by the blockchain, and a _**Withdraw Ticket**_ is created for you.

4\) To see your Withdraw ticket, go to the _**Withdraw**_ section.
