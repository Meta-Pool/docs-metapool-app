Meta Pool allows Multi-chain staking: **$IP, $ETH, $NEAR, $AURORA, $WNEAR, $ICP and $SOL (mSOL, bSOL, jitoSOL, HubSOL)** token holders can stake and earn rewards, while also providing an **easy way to unstake with no lockup period (fast unstake).**

### Steps to follow <a href="#f155" id="f155"></a>

1\) Go to [https://www.metapool.app/](https://www.metapool.app/) an then to the Stake section. Or directly to: [https://www.metapool.app/stake](https://www.metapool.app/stake).

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2866%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2\) **Token selection:** Choose the token of your preference. Note that by default the staking is in the $IP token, but make sure to select the desired token and that the Stake option is selected.


<Aside type="note">

**Here you can simulate a transaction, without having to connect your wallet.**

</Aside>


3\) **Connect your wallet** by clicking the Connect wallet button.


<Aside type="note">

The wallet you connect will depend on the network to which the selected token belongs. Once you click Connect your Wallet, a dialog box opens with the options for connecting wallets.

![](/assets/image%20%2867%29.png)

</Aside>


4\) **Choose your preferred wallet** according to the token preselected befor, for this example, we will use [Meteor Wallet](https://wallet.meteorwallet.app/) for the $NEAR token.

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2869%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5\) **Enter the amount** that you wish to stake, then click '_**Stake**_' according to the Token selected.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2872%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


<Aside type="note">

Note, the **minimum** you are allowed to stake according to the token selected:

* 0.10 $IP
* 1.00 $NEAR
* 0.50 $wNEAR
* 1.00 $AURORA
* 0.01 $ETH
* 1.00 $Q
* 0.10 $ICP
* 0.01 $SOL, $mSOL, $bSOL, $jitoSOL, $hubSOL

</Aside>



<Aside type="caution">

Note: always leave some $IP or $NEAR or $ETH or $Q or $ICP or $SOL (depending to the Token selected) on your wallet to cover transaction costs.

</Aside>


6\) **$AURORA and $wNEAR Permission and Fee:** To operate with $AURORA and $wNEAR, the selected wallet will request permission to be provided in order to stake. This is done by approving the amount to be authorized in a transaction prior to stake approval. Therefore, two actions are required to stake.&#x20;

* **Approve:** Enter the desired amount to stake. Then press the "Approve AURORA" button.

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2874%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

* In your wallet, enter the amount for which you want to give permission to the application. It must be equal to or greater than the amount to be staked.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2875%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


<Aside type="note">

Only enter a number that you´re comfortable with Meta Pool spending now or in the future. You can always increase the spending cap later.&#x20;

</Aside>


* If you choose the "**Use site suggestion**" amount, the system will incorporate the maximum possible amount that will allow you to interact without being asked for this permission again. \
  If you choose the "**Max**" button, the system will automatically complete with the maximum balance you have of this asset (in this case $AUR token). \
  But you can complete the field with the desired amount, taking into account that it must always be equal to or greater than the amount for which you wish to carry out the transaction.
* **Fee**: Keep in mind that **ONLY** staking with the $wNEAR token involves a small fee of 0.1%.

7\) **Stake**: click on the "**Stake now**" button and "_**Approve**_" or "_**Confirm**_" the transaction in your wallet.&#x20;

<figure><img src="/assets/image%20%2877%29.png" alt="" /><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


<Aside type="note">

You'll get **confirmation** after the transaction has been processed by the blockchain and your stake deposit has been successful. This usually only _**takes a few seconds**_.

</Aside>


8\) **Dashboard**: Now you can find your accounts holdings on the Dashboard section. [https://www.metapool.app/dashboard](https://www.metapool.app/dashboard)
